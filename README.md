   - WHY? -
   
   I genuinely have a hatred for big tech companies. Sure, many of them were fine at first:
   
   Google was a simple search engine in the 2000s, their slogan quite literally was "don't be
   evil". In 2025, Google ended its pledge not to use AI for weapons and surveillance.
   
   OpenAI used to be a cool nonprofit startup. Now they buy and sell your data, make their
   models as flattering as possible to hook users on ChatGPT as a companion, not as a tool.
   On January 10, 2024, its "usage policies" removed the ban on "activity that has 
   high risk of physical harm", aka war and weapons and surveillance.
   
   I don't have much to say against AI and LLMs as a technology. When used AS A TOOL, they
   can really be useful. However, it shouldn't replace anyone. It should help and not take.
   Is it human? No. So don't treat it like a human! Sure, it is trained on human-made data
   but still, it is NOT human. There are cases of people actually treating ChatGPT (for
   example) as a companion (sending it "i landed" at the end of a flight, having regular
   conversations with it about work, people, etc. using AI on advertisements instead of
   paying a money to a human who can draw well) instead of a tool. AI shouldn't be a partner,
   it should be a hammer. It shouldn't be your therapist, it should be an axe.
   An auto-complete.
   
   So now, let's say you read this and be like "Wow! Big Tech isn't up to any good and AI is
   actually okay sometimes!". Where do you go use this technology? claude.ai of course! Oh?
   It needs your phone number? Too bad! Try chatgpt.com! It collects too much data? It's too
